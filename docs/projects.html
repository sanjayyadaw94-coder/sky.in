01 / SENTINEL-1

Plot-wise SAR rice water-status monitoring

Use radar observations and crop-stage context to evaluate potential wet/dry transitions across field polygons.

Question
How might field-scale surface-water status change between satellite passes?
Approach
Align Sentinel-1 C-band VV/VH observations with plot geometries, acquisition dates and crop-stage information. Estimate plot summaries against a defensible baseline.
Potential outputs
A dated plot summary, possible status changes, coverage flags and ground-check priorities.
Interpretation & QA
SAR backscatter is influenced by crop structure, roughness and moisture; wet/dry classes require local ground-truth validation and cannot be guaranteed 100% accurate.

02 / SENTINEL-2

Seasonal crop condition using spectral indices

Compare cloud-filtered vegetation and moisture-sensitive indices to describe crop patterns over time.

Question
Which areas show relative changes in vegetation greenness or canopy moisture?
Approach
Process Sentinel-2 surface reflectance with cloud masking; derive NDVI and NDMI, summarize by month or field, and compare consistent observation windows.
Potential outputs
Seasonal index maps, simple trend summaries, area statistics and field-visit questions.
Interpretation & QA
Indices are indicators, not a stand-alone diagnosis of yield, irrigation or disease; clouds, season and crop type affect interpretation.

03 / KOBO & GIS

Field survey GPS and photo validation

Connect digital submissions, geo-tagged photos and plot polygons with a reproducible data-quality review.

Question
Do field-submission coordinates and plot identifiers agree with the mapped records?
Approach
Check submission timestamps and plot IDs, inspect EXIF/visible geo-tags when available, verify coordinate systems and compare locations with corresponding polygons.
Potential outputs
An exception register showing missing coordinates, duplicate IDs, spatial mismatches and records needing manual review.
Interpretation & QA
Do not invent missing coordinates. Image metadata can be absent or imprecise; ambiguous OCR or conflicting evidence must be flagged.
