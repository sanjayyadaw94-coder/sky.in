01 / OPTICAL IMAGERY

Agricultural satellite vegetation indices

My optical satellite analysis covers vegetation, chlorophyll-related indicators and moisture indices with Sentinel-2 MSI and Landsat 8/9 OLI-family imagery.

Question
Which areas show relative changes in vegetation greenness or canopy moisture?
Approach
Apply cloud masking and suitable surface-reflectance corrections to Sentinel-2 MultiSpectral Instrument (MSI), Landsat 8 Operational Land Imager (OLI) and Landsat 9 Operational Land Imager 2 (OLI-2) data; calculate compatible vegetation and moisture indices.
Potential outputs
Seasonal index maps, simple trend summaries, area statistics and field-visit questions.
Interpretation & QA
Indices are indicators, not direct measurements of yield or nutrients. Red-edge indices need dedicated bands available in Sentinel-2 MSI but not Landsat 8/9 OLI-family sensors.

02 / TERRAIN ANALYSIS

Digital elevation and terrain analysis

GIS analysis of topography, terrain derivatives and landform patterns using elevation datasets.

Focus
Interpret terrain and elevation differences across a mapped area.
Methods
Digital Elevation Model (DEM), Digital Terrain Model (DTM) and Digital Surface Model (DSM) interpretation with GIS tools.
Analysis outputs
Elevation maps, slope, aspect, hillshade, drainage context and watershed-oriented terrain analysis.
Quality notes
DTM typically denotes bare-earth terrain; DSM includes surface features. DEM is often a general elevation term, and meaning depends on source data.

03 / UAV IMAGERY

Drone / UAV imagery data analysis

Analysis of high-resolution imagery from an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) for spatial interpretation and agricultural monitoring.

Identify visible agricultural and land features at high spatial detail.
Methods
UAV imagery and orthomosaic interpretation, image classification and spatial overlays; where suitable inputs exist, analysis of photogrammetric surface products.
Analysis outputs
Feature delineation, agricultural image interpretation, land-cover patterns and spatial comparisons.

04 / GIS DATA

Field survey GPS and photo validation

Connect digital submissions, geo-tagged photos and plot polygons with a reproducible data-quality review.

Question
Do field-submission coordinates and plot identifiers agree with the mapped records?
Approach
Check submission timestamps and plot IDs, inspect EXIF/visible geo-tags when available, verify coordinate systems and compare locations with corresponding polygons.
Potential outputs
An exception register showing missing coordinates, duplicate IDs, spatial mismatches and records needing manual review.
Interpretation & QA
Do not invent missing coordinates. Image metadata can be absent or imprecise; ambiguous OCR or conflicting evidence must be flagged.
